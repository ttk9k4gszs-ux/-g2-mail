g2-mail Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

What this app is

g2-mail is a personal tool used by a single user (its owner) to read and send email from one dedicated Gmail account, displayed on Even Realities G2 smart glasses.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app accesses Gmail messages of the dedicated account through the Gmail API, using only these scopes: read messages (gmail.readonly) and send messages (gmail.send). The app cannot delete messages.

How data is handled

  • Requests pass through a Cloudflare Worker operated by the owner. The Worker is stateless: it stores no email content, keeps no logs of message bodies, and holds no database.
  • Email content is shown only on the owner's own devices (the glasses plugin and its companion phone UI).
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Credentials

OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's own Cloudflare account. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately disables all access.

Contact

The owner can be reached at the Gmail account that authorized this app.
